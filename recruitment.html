How to Qualify

PLAY REAL. EARN TRUST.

01

Invite friends

Share your link. The invited player creates an account and confirms your referral. Each recruit may credit only one recruiter.

02

Help them stick around

A recruit must play at least six verified hours on three separate days within their first fourteen days on the DayZ server.

03

Earn consideration

High-ranking participants may be considered for a moderator interview or a 7-day trial. Final staff decisions belong to server ownership.

Fake accounts, self-referrals, fabricated check-ins and other attempts to manipulate rankings may result in disqualification. Player activity is verified by staff; the website does not automatically access Host Havoc logs.
